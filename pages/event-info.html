FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much does it cost?

Listing your event is absolutely FREE! We only charge a 10-15% commission on tickets sold through our platform.

How long until my event is live?

After sending your email, our team typically reviews and activates events within 48-72 hours after verification.

What images do I need?

A high-quality event poster/cover image (minimum 1200x600px) is required. Additional images are welcome.

When do I get paid?

Payouts are processed within 7-10 business days after your event ends. Funds are transferred to your bank account.

Can I sell VIP tickets?

Yes! You can create multiple ticket tiers (General, VIP, Early Bird) with different prices and benefits.

Can I cancel my event?

Yes, you can cancel anytime before the event goes live. After listing, cancellation policies apply.
